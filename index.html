About

My story

Fifteen years in software and finance: McKinsey, then product at two startups that got acquired.

Then I founded Open Minds Early School in Oakland with my wife, Karen Hernández.

Running a school teaches you where it breaks. Teaching is hard work; growing a school and keeping it solvent is harder. In Venezuela it is brutal: families want to pay and the money cannot get through. So we built UnoPago, the financial operating system for schools there: payments, billing and reconciliation in one place. A school that can collect is a school that can plan.

Before all this: aerospace engineering at MIT, economics at the Kennedy School.

Plenty of failures, a few things that worked, and something new to learn every day.
